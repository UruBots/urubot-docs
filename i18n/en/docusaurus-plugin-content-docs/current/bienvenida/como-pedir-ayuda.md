# How to ask for help

Working in robotics means running into new errors and problems all the time. Asking for help in time avoids frustration and helps the whole team improve the documentation.

---

## The "Ask for help" form

The formal channel to register a need is the **Ask for help** form available on the portal. You can use it for any of these situations:

- **Technical question:** A concept or command doesn't work as the guide says.
- **I need training:** You want to learn a specific topic (Git, ROS 2, Linux) and need guidance.
- **I need a computer / equipment:** You don't have your own PC, or yours can't handle certain tools, and you need to use a lab machine.
- **I need a practice partner:** You'd rather do the exercises in pairs to learn together.
- **I have a proposal or idea:** You want to propose a project, improvement or experiment.
- **I found an error in a document:** A guide has a broken link or a confusing step.
- **I need access to a platform:** Permissions for a repository, simulator or tool.
- **Blocked on a task:** You took a task in Deck and can't make progress.

---

## What happens after submitting the form?

1. **Human review:** Coordination or an area mentor receives the request, evaluates it and gets in touch with you.
2. **No automatic assignments:** The portal's AI can suggest readings from the library, but it **never** assigns computers, permissions or physical resources on its own. Every assignment requires coordination's confirmation.
3. **Transparent follow-up:** If your request leads to a concrete action, a task is created in Deck or it's added as an item for the next meeting.

---

## WhatsApp group for active members

For quick announcements, coordinating lab arrival times or brief day-to-day questions, the team uses the WhatsApp group for active members.

- Keep messages focused on team topics.
- Don't share other people's personal data or confidential information.
- If your question needs follow-up or resource assignment, always use the formal form in addition to the informal message.
