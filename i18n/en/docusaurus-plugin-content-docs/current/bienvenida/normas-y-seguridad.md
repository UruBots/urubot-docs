# Rules and safety

Working in robotics combines delicate hardware, high-power batteries and software under development. Keeping people and equipment safe is the number one priority.

---

## Physical safety and robot use

1. **Mandatory supervision:** Never power on, move actuators or run code on a real robot without the explicit presence and authorization of a mentor or coordinator.
2. **Test and simulation environments first:** Every algorithm must first be tested in a simulator or with unit tests before being transferred to physical hardware.
3. **Emergency stop:** Before powering any mobile platform or robotic arm, identify where the physical emergency stop (e-stop) switch is.
4. **Batteries and power:** Don't leave batteries charging unattended. If there's any swelling, strange smell or abnormal heating, disconnect the power and report it immediately.

---

## Software and data safety

- **Isolated practice environments:** Learning exercises are done on personal branches or your own practice repositories. Don't touch main branches or production databases.
- **No secrets in code:** Never upload passwords, GitHub tokens or access keys to any repository.
- **Privacy among teammates:** Members' contact details, academic profiles and help requests are private. Don't share them outside authorized channels.

---

## Coexistence and workspace

- **Mutual respect:** We foster an environment where asking questions is not embarrassing and mistakes are learning opportunities.
- **Lab order:** Every tool, cable or component you use must go back to its place at the end of the day.
- **Clean desk:** Leave the work table clear for the next teammate who comes to work.
