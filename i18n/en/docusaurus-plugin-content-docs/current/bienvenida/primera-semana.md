# Your first week at UruBots

So you don't feel lost or disoriented, here's a suggested day-by-day guide for your first seven days on the team.

You can go at your own pace; it's not a race.

---

## Suggested 7-day plan

### Day 1: Welcome and onboarding
- Fill out the **Member registration** form with your basic details and availability.
- Read the [Start here](../intro.md) page.
- If you don't have your own computer, indicate it in the form to coordinate access to a lab PC or to be assigned a study partner.

### Day 2: Get to know the paths
- Explore the [What is UruBots?](./que-es-urubots.md) guide and the [Rules and safety](./normas-y-seguridad.md) guide.
- Open the [Path: First steps](../rutas/primeros-pasos.md) to get a clear map of the training stages.

### Day 3: Your first contact with Git
- Enter the [Git and GitHub from scratch](../rutas/git-y-github-desde-cero.md) path.
- You can do the basic practice either from a computer or from your phone's browser by creating your own test repository.

### Day 4: Pick your first microtask
- Enter the **Deck** app (*UruBots general training* board).
- Look at the list of tasks in the **Available** column.
- Assign yourself an initial task (for example, "My first contribution with GitHub" or "Welcome reading from your phone") and move it to the **In progress** column.

### Day 5: Solve it and ask if you get stuck
- Work on your microtask following the instructions.
- If you run into a problem or don't know how to continue, fill out the [Ask for help](./como-pedir-ayuda.md) form. Never just wait on your own!

### Day 6: Delivery and review
- When your deliverable is ready (a link, a short text or a solved exercise), document it in the task comments in Deck.
- Move the task from the **In progress** column to **In review**. A mentor will review your work and give you constructive feedback.

### Day 7: Connect with the team
- Read the summary or minutes of the team's last meeting under [Meetings](../reuniones.md).
- If there's a meeting this week, join in to meet your teammates and see what projects they're working on.
