# UruBots meetings: Dynamics and minutes

At UruBots, meetings are brief, agreement-oriented and focused on unblocking technical or coordination difficulties.

Here we explain how to participate, how to propose topics and how to catch up if you couldn't attend.

---

## 1. How to propose an agenda topic

Any member can suggest a topic to discuss at the next meeting:

- **Web form:** Fill out the **Propose meeting topic** form available on the portal.
- **Suggested deadline:** Send your proposal at least 24 hours in advance so coordination can organize the agenda.
- **Recommended topics:** Blockers affecting several people, proposals for new projects, purchase or repair needs, and calendar agreements.

---

## 2. Where are the minutes stored?

- All official minutes are archived in this documentation section under the format `YYYY-MM-DD-reunion.md` following the [Meeting template](./plantillas/plantilla-reunion.md).
- Each set of minutes lists the topics covered, the decisions made and the tasks assigned in Nextcloud Deck with their owner.

---

## 3. Missed a meeting? Catch up in 3 steps

If you couldn't attend an in-person or virtual meeting, don't worry:

1. **Read the WhatsApp summary:** An official 4-to-8-line summary is published in the active members WhatsApp group at the end of each meeting.
2. **Check the decisions table:** Open the corresponding minutes in the documentation and look only at the **Decisions and agreements** table.
3. **Check your tasks in Deck:** See if you have any new assigned card or if any of your help requests was addressed.
