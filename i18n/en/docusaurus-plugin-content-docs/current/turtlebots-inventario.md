## Fault or maintenance reporting protocol

When a TurtleBot has a mechanical, electronic or calibration fault:

1. Turn off the robot and carefully remove the battery.
2. Leave a physical label on the robot indicating the fault.
3. Update this table in the documentation, changing the status to *Under maintenance*.
4. Fill out the **Ask for help** form (type: *Maintenance or error report*) to coordinate the repair with the hardware team.
