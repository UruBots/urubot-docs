# Start here: your starting point in the UruBots Portal

Welcome to the **UruBots Portal**! This is the team's collaborative space for learning, coordinating tasks and sharing knowledge.

It doesn't matter if you're just starting out or already have experience: no one is born knowing this stuff, and we learn as a team.

---

## In 3 steps: the essentials to get started

To get going without complications, follow these three shortcuts:

1. **Your initial path:**
   Read the [Path: First steps in UruBots](./rutas/primeros-pasos.md). It guides you step by step so you understand the dynamics and make your first contribution without stress.

2. **Your first task:**
   Check the microtasks on the **UruBots general training** board (defined in `tareas-iniciales.yaml` or from the Deck app). Pick a task from the **Available** column, move it to **In progress** and start exploring.

3. **How to ask for help:**
   Check the [How to ask for help](./bienvenida/como-pedir-ayuda.md) guide. If something is unclear or you get stuck, asking in time is part of doing good work.

---

## Don't have a computer?

Don't worry. Not having your own equipment is not a barrier to joining:

- **Make progress from your phone:** Many initial activities (reading guides, Git concepts, proposing ideas and reviewing documentation) can be done 100% from your phone's browser.
- **Request a PC or work in pairs:** If you need a lab computer or prefer to practice in pairs with a teammate, request it via the "Ask for help" form.
- **Human confirmation:** No one confirms equipment for you automatically, and no AI does it either; lab computer assignments are confirmed by team coordination based on physical availability.
