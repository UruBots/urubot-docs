# Learning path: First steps in UruBots

This is the main welcome path. Its goal is for anyone just joining to be able to find their way around, understand the working dynamics and make their first documented contribution.

---

## Path card

- **Name:** First steps in UruBots
- **Goal:** For a newcomer to find their way around and make their first documented contribution.
- **Level:** Initial / Beginner
- **Prerequisites:** None. A desire to learn and collaborate.
- **Required device:** You can complete it from a phone or a computer.

---

## Thematic units

### Unit 1: What UruBots is and how we work
Learn about the team's purpose, the active projects and the culture of open collaboration.
- **Reading:** [What is UruBots?](../bienvenida/que-es-urubots.md)

### Unit 2: Lab rules and requesting help
Learn the fundamental physical and digital safety guidelines, and how to raise your hand when something doesn't work.
- **Readings:** [Rules and safety](../bienvenida/normas-y-seguridad.md) and [How to ask for help](../bienvenida/como-pedir-ayuda.md)

### Unit 3: Git and GitHub from scratch
Understand what version control is and practice creating a repository and making commits from your computer or your phone's browser.
- **Associated path:** [Git and GitHub from scratch](./git-y-github-desde-cero.md)

### Unit 4: Choose an area of interest
Explore the different specialties (Autonomous cars, RoboCup @Home, educational TurtleBots or Software tools) to decide which one you'd like to go deeper into.
- **Suggested readings:** [Autonomous car fundamentals](./autos-autonomos-fundamentos.md) and [Getting to know the RoboCup @Home platform](./robocup-athome-conocer-plataforma.md)

### Unit 5: Complete a microtask with evidence
Pick an initial task on the **UruBots general training** Deck board, solve it in your environment and document the result.
- **Resource:** General training Deck board (initial tasks).

### Unit 6: Share progress and receive feedback
Move your task to review, notify your mentor and receive constructive feedback on your work.
- **Associated guide:** [Your first week at UruBots](../bienvenida/primera-semana.md)

---

## Completion criteria

The path is considered complete when:
1. You've read the basic welcome and rules concepts.
2. You've delivered an initial microtask with evidence verified by a mentor.
3. You know how to use the help request channel when needed.
